Presentation & Polish

This is a solid, well-organised seminar report with a genuinely good literature survey. The four-paper selection is coherent and progressive (holistic CTI management → neural-network detection → real-time smart-home deployment → blockchain-based collaborative sharing), the taxonomy figure and comparison table add real value, and the writing demonstrates clear understanding of CTI lifecycles, MISP, IDS/IPS, and AI/ML roles. However, the document is undermined by a large number of typographical, spacing, and sentence-truncation defects — including visible LaTeX rendering artifacts and incomplete reference entries — that must be fixed before submission. Most issues are mechanical and fixable within a few hours.

Strengths

  • Strong survey architecture. The four papers are well-chosen: they cover complementary axes (framework, algorithm, practical deployment, collaboration), and the taxonomy diagram (Fig. 2.0.1) makes the survey logic immediately clear.
  • Good comparative analysis (Ch. 3). The discussion correctly distinguishes detection approaches, intelligence sources, and the distinct roles of AI/ML, MISP/IDS-IPS, and blockchain — this is the strongest analytical section.
  • Practical grounding. Paper [3]'s Raspberry Pi smart-home implementation is highlighted well, and the challenges chapter honestly addresses resource constraints, false positives/negatives, and TI quality.
  • Complete front matter. Certificate, declaration, acknowledgement, abstract, abbreviations list, and figures/tables lists are all present and appropriately ordered.
  • Clear, readable prose style at the paragraph level; technical terms (IoC, TTP, SIEM) are used correctly.
